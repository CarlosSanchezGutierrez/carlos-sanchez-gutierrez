## About

I am a Computer Science and Technology student at Tecnológico de Monterrey, expected to graduate in **June 2027**. My work spans data engineering, cloud architecture, applied AI and product development.

I start with the operational constraint: what needs to happen, where the data comes from, what can fail, and who depends on the result. Then I connect the data model, architecture and workflow to something dependable, explainable and useful in practice. Traceability and adoption matter as much as the code.
